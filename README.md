# PolyTrack Ranked

**[Play PolyTrack Ranked](https://staticquasar931.github.io/PolyTrack-0.6.3-Ranked/)** in your browser. No install required.

A Ranked mod for Kodub's PolyTrack 0.6.3. Chase personal bests, race ghosts, and see how you stack up across official, community, and Extra Tracks.

- **Rankings and Route Planner:** compare results and find your next track.
- **Events:** play daily and weekly challenges, Kodub's Track of the Week, and Rolling Hills Racer. Event RP is separate from Overall RP.
- **Achievements, Racer Studio, and Garage:** unlock styles and customize your racer and car.

Public standings are published snapshots. A local PB can appear before the next update, but Event RP is only awarded after verification.

Use **Help & Controls** in-game for shortcuts and bug reports. Browse the [features and credits](https://staticquasar931.github.io/PolyTrack-0.6.3-Ranked/about.html) or join the [community Discord](https://discord.gg/jW2az4AQUH).
